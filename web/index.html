A black box for autonomous drones that the operator cannot edit, hide or forge.

Every flight goes into a QoreChain rollup. The drone signs it with ML-DSA-87, the records are sealed with ML-KEM-1024 so only the investigator and insurer can read them, every batch is anchored on QoreChain, and a watchtower re-checks each one and pays victims when a safety rule was broken.

  1. Fly the drone signs its records
  2. Seal ML-KEM-1024 to investigator + insurer
  3. Settle batches anchored under ML-DSA-87
  4. Watch re-execute, judge, challenge fraud
  5. Pay withdrawals on QoreChain
