Sky
Proof

A post-quantum black box for autonomous drones.

When an autonomous drone hurts someone, the only flight log belongs to the operator. SkyProof seals every flight into a QoreChain rollup that no operator can edit, hide or forge, and pays the victim when a safety rule was broken.

System status
    The arena · real simulator and rules, running in your browser

    Watch a drone decide.

    01 · Fly & sign

    Every move needs a fresh approval.

    Before each take-off, drop and landing the drone asks its safety gate. It acts only on an ALLOW bound to that exact request; each record is hash-chained and every segment is signed with ML-DSA-87.

    02 · Seal & settle

    A black box nobody can quietly edit.

    Segments are sealed with ML-KEM-1024 to the investigator and insurer and posted to an x/rdk rollup. Each batch is anchored on QoreChain under the creator's ML-DSA-87 signature.

    03 · Watch & pay

    Broken rule? The victim is paid.

    The watchtower re-executes every batch and challenges fraud. It opens claimed flights, applies six rules, and pays the victim from the operator's bond as a withdrawal on QoreChain.
