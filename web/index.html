SkyProof · live on QoreChain testnet

A black box for delivery drones that nobody can tamper with.

If a drone breaks a safety rule and hurts someone, SkyProof proves it and pays the victim automatically. Not even the drone company can edit, hide or fake the record.

    The drone asks before every risky move

    Before taking off, dropping a parcel or landing, the drone asks a safety check. It may only go ahead on a fresh "Yes".

    ML-DSA-87 signed, hash-chained records

    2

    Everything is locked in a black box

    Each part of the flight is sealed and stored on a blockchain. Only the investigator and the insurer can open it, and nobody can change it later.

    ML-KEM-1024 sealing · QoreChain x/rdk rollup

    3

    Broken rule? The victim is paid

    An automatic checker reads the record against six safety rules. If one was broken, the victim is paid from the operator's deposit. No lawyers, no waiting.

    Cheating caughtFraud proofs upheld

    Needs a keyOpen a sealed black box (investigator)
